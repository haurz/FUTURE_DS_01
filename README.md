# Task 1: Business Sales Performance Analytics (`FUTURE_DS_01`)

## Executive Summary
This project evaluates transactional sales performance across regions, product categories, and temporal trends to identify revenue drivers and bottom-line margin optimization opportunities.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Revenue**: $2,297,200.86
- **Total Net Profit**: $286,397.02
- **Net Profit Margin**: 12.47%
- **Average Order Value (AOV)**: $458.61

## Core Business Insights
1. **Seasonal Volume Peaks**: Sales revenue experiences strong Q4 surges (October–December) driven by holiday demand, followed by predictable early Q1 contractions.
2. **Category Profitability Disparities**: While **Technology** generates the highest gross volume, **Office Supplies** yields superior margin stability due to lower fulfillment overhead.
3. **Regional Discounting Impact**: High sales volume in specific regions does not correlate directly with net margin due to aggressive promotional discounting strategies.

## Strategic Recommendations
- **Margin Protection Rules**: Establish a maximum discount threshold per region to avoid profit erosion on high-volume transactions.
- **Inventory Optimization**: Scale up supply chain allocations by late Q3 to satisfy predictable Q4 demand spikes without incurring rush shipping charges.
- **Cross-Selling Focus**: Focus marketing budget on expanding high-margin sub-categories (e.g., Paper, Envelopes) alongside high-revenue hardware products.

## Technology Stack
- **Data Processing**: Python (Pandas)
- **Data Visualization**: Plotly Express / Plotly Graph Objects
- **Environment**: Jupyter Notebook / VS Code
